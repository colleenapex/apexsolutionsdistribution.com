Questions Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from brands exploring a wholesale distribution partnership with Apex Solutions.

Apex Solutions Distribution LLC is a wholesale purchasing and reseller partner specializing in consumer goods across Amazon and multi-channel marketplaces. We focus on sourcing authorized inventory and representing brands as a compliant reseller — supporting marketplace visibility and distribution reach for the brands we work with.
We focus on replenishable consumer goods — products that customers purchase repeatedly across a broad range of everyday categories including household essentials, health and beauty, personal care, grocery, and more. These high-velocity, repeat-purchase categories perform well in an authorized wholesale distribution model on Amazon and similar platforms.
When engaged as an authorized distribution partner, we conduct periodic marketplace reviews to identify potential unauthorized sellers, gray market listings, and pricing irregularities. We document findings and coordinate through appropriate reporting channels to help preserve your brand's reputation and pricing structure — with the scope of monitoring aligned to the terms of our partnership.
Use the contact form below to submit your inquiry, or email our purchasing department directly at purchasing@ApexSolutionsDistribution.com. We encourage you to include your brand catalog or product line sheet — the form accepts file attachments. Our onboarding team will evaluate your submission and respond within one to two business days.
Exclusivity terms vary by brand and category. We evaluate each partnership individually based on product type, market conditions, and the scope of distribution support required. Our primary requirement is that we operate as an authorized reseller — this is what enables us to maintain pricing integrity and platform compliance on your behalf.
We are selective with the brands we represent — which means every partner gets dedicated attention, real-time communication, and a tailored distribution approach. Our focus on replenishable goods, authorized sourcing, and active brand protection sets us apart from general resellers who treat every product the same way.
